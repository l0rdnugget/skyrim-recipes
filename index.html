About This Page

Skyrim is a game I keep coming back to, usually when life gets stressful. There is something cozy about it: the snowy mountains, the taverns with a fire going, and a story I know well enough that it feels like comfort instead of a challenge. Years ago I bought The Elder Scrolls: The Official Cookbook and never actually cooked from it. This page is my excuse to finally use it.

My featured recipe is the sweet roll, the most famous food in the game. I am also including skooma, Skyrim's infamous purple drink, made here as a harmless fruit mocktail, and a hearty apple cabbage stew as a main dish. Together they make a full tavern meal.

Sweet Rolls

Soft, lightly sweet yeast rolls baked in a bundt-style pan and finished with a thick vanilla glaze.

Makes 12 rolls · Prep 30 min · Rise 1 hr 45 min · Bake 18–22 min

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 1 cup whole milk, warmed to about 110°F
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons (1 packet) active dry yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for kneading

For the glaze

  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, melted

Directions

  1. Pour the warm milk into a large bowl and stir in the yeast and 1 teaspoon of the sugar. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes, until the top is foamy.
  2. Add the rest of the sugar, the melted butter, the eggs, and the salt. Whisk until everything is combined.
  3. Stir in the flour one cup at a time until a soft dough forms and pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
  4. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and knead for 6 to 8 minutes, until it is smooth and springs back when you press it.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a towel, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 1 hour, until doubled in size.
  6. Grease a 12-cup mini bundt pan or a standard muffin tin. Punch down the dough and divide it into 12 equal pieces.
  7. Roll each piece into a rope about 8 inches long, pinch the ends together to form a ring, and press it into a cup of the pan.
  8. Cover the pan and let the rolls rise again for 30 to 45 minutes, until puffy. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 350°F.
  9. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until the tops are golden brown. Let the rolls cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack.
  10. Whisk the powdered sugar, milk, vanilla, and melted butter into a thick glaze. Spoon it over the cooled rolls and let it drip down the sides.

Tip: Let the rolls cool completely before glazing, or the glaze will melt and run off. Sweet rolls are best the day they are made, but they keep in an airtight container for up to two days.

Skooma

In the game, skooma is a forbidden substance sold in a purple bottle. This version is a sweet, fizzy, completely legal fruit drink with the same deep purple color.

Makes 2 servings · Prep 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grape juice
  • 1/2 cup blackberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 cup sparkling water, chilled
  • Ice cubes

Directions

  1. In a glass or shaker, mash the blackberries with the honey and lemon juice until the berries break down.
  2. Stir in the grape juice, then strain the mixture to remove the seeds.
  3. Fill two glasses with ice, divide the juice mixture between them, and top each glass with sparkling water.
  4. Stir gently and serve right away, ideally in a small glass bottle for the full Skyrim effect.

Apple Cabbage Stew

A simple, filling stew that shows up in cooking pots all over Skyrim. Sweet apples balance the cabbage, and smoked sausage makes it a full meal.

Makes 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 12 ounces smoked sausage, sliced into rounds
  • 1/2 head green cabbage, chopped (about 6 cups)
  • 2 apples, peeled and chopped
  • 2 medium potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until soft.
  2. Add the sausage and cook for another 5 minutes, stirring, until lightly browned.
  3. Stir in the cabbage, apples, and potatoes, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the cabbage starts to wilt.
  4. Pour in the broth and add the thyme, salt, and pepper. Bring the pot to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
  6. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, taste, and add more salt if needed. Serve hot with crusty bread.
